# The Worktree Choir

Each morning the board fills up with small, clear asks,
and one by one the agents claim their tasks.
In separate worktrees, side by side they write,
no branch on top of another's, no merge-day fight.
They tick each step and leave a note behind,
so anyone who follows reads their mind.
When something's unclear, they stop and ask, not guess,
and wait for the principal to answer yes.
At dusk the work comes home for one last look:
the principal reads each change like pages of a book,
then merges what is ready, sends back what is not,
and the team begins again with what they've got.
